Connection and usage tips

Connect the left USB/OTG port to your computer. Connect your phone to the key-agent hotspot, then open http://192.168.4.1. If your phone reports no internet access, choose to stay connected.

Press BOOT to stop all running sequences, or start all enabled sequences when paused. Power-on, USB reconnection, and host sleep leave all sequences paused. Closing this page or disconnecting your phone does not stop a running sequence.

Saving stops only the selected sequence and releases its key; other sequences continue. Restarting begins at the first action. Editing does not send keys. Labels use the US keyboard layout; output depends on your computer keyboard layout and input method. Use Cmd/Win for Mac Command.
